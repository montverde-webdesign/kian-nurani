About me

I started learning web design because I wanted to understand how websites work. I now understand that nobody really knows why the code works, we just keep adding things till the screen looks right. My favorite part of building websites is watching a blank page turn into something impressive, usually after several emotional negotiations with HTML. I’m getting better at HTML and CSS, although CSS seems to have a personal vendetta against me. I’m still learning, but at least websites are starting to look intentional instead of accidental.
