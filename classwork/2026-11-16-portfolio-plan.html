About me (first draft)

My intro line: I build websites, break websites, and occasionally manage to do both at the same time.

I started learning web design because I wanted to understand how websites work. I now understand that nobody really knows why the code works, we just keep adding things till the screen looks right. My favorite part of building websites is watching a blank page turn into something impressive, usually after several emotional negotiations with HTML. I’m getting better at HTML and CSS, although CSS seems to have a personal vendetta against me. My best project so far is Neon Tide, where I designed the ticket section and footer. Apparently, making a $299 ticket look appealing is also a form of graphic design. I’m still learning, but at least websites are starting to look intentional instead of accidental.

My style idea

  • My site should feel: Bold, Playful, Retro
  • Main color: #C6FF00
  • Accent color: #FF00A8
  • Background color: #FFFFFF
  • Heading font: Bangers
  • Body font: Rubik
  • Why: I chose Bangers for my headings and Rubik for body text because apparently regular fonts weren't causing enough chaos. I went with radioactive lime, and neon pink to create an aggressive arcade vibe. I wanted my website to look like a bowling alley had a disagreement with a radioactive highlighter.
